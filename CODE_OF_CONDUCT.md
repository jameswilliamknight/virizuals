# Community conduct

Be kind, specific and constructive. Discuss the work without attacking people.
Welcome contributors regardless of background, identity or experience, and
respect different accessibility needs and ways of participating.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualised conduct, deliberate
disruption and publishing another person's private information are not welcome.
These expectations apply in issues, pull requests and other project spaces.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, lock discussions, or restrict
participation to protect the community. Responses should be proportionate to the
behaviour and its impact. Good-faith mistakes are an opportunity to learn.

For misconduct, use GitHub's **Report content** action on the relevant content.
Avoid repeating private information in a public report. The maintainer will
respect the privacy of people involved as far as the platform allows.
